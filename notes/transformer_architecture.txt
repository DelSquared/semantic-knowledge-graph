Transformer Architecture Details

The Transformer architecture replaces recurrence with self-attention to compute representations of input sequences in parallel. Each layer contains two sub-layers: a multi-head self-attention mechanism and a position-wise fully-connected feedforward network. Residual connections around each sub-layer and layer normalization are essential for training deep stacks of these layers effectively. The self-attention mechanism computes attention scores between all positions in the input, allowing each token to attend to all other tokens in the sequence.

The attention computation uses three learned linear projections (query, key, value) to transform input vectors. The scaled dot-product attention scales the dot products by the square root of the dimension to prevent the softmax from becoming too peaked. Multi-head attention extends this by running several attention operations in parallel and concatenating their outputs, enabling the model to jointly attend to information from different representation subspaces at different positions.
